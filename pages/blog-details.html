STILLWATER LAUNDRY

Give every load the care it deserves.

Everyday care · 6 minute read · Stillwater Journal

A good laundry routine starts before you press the start button. A few simple checks help you choose the right machine and avoid unnecessary re-washing.

Start with the label, then the load.

Look at each care label before combining items. Separate dark and light colors, and put delicate pieces aside if their temperature or drying instructions differ.

Leave room in the drum

A tightly packed washer cannot move water and detergent freely through the fabric. Choose the next drum size for bulky pieces, rather than pushing them into a small machine.

  • 8 kg: everyday clothing and light towels.
  • 14 kg: family loads and heavier linen.
  • 20 kg: washable bedding with plenty of movement.
Room to move is just as important as the capacity number on the machine.

Use detergent thoughtfully.

Follow the detergent pack’s guidance for load size and soil level. More detergent does not automatically mean cleaner laundry; too much can leave residue.

Dry for the fabric, not the clock.

Check the tumble-dry symbol first. Choose low heat for appropriate delicate items and avoid mixing fabrics with very different drying times. Check halfway through and remove pieces that are already dry.

Let bedding dry completely

Bulky washable bedding may need more than one drying interval. Pause to redistribute it and make sure the filling is thoroughly dry before storing. Always follow the manufacturer’s instructions.

THE FRESH-FINISH CHECKLIST

Three small habits worth keeping.

  1. Read the care label. The manufacturer’s washing and drying instructions come first.
  2. Give fabrics room. Choose by bulk and keep below the drum’s fill line.
  3. Check before adding heat. Remove dry items and redistribute the rest.
